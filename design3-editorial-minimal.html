Principles
we don't

i.
Clarity over cleverness

ii.
Speed is a feature
We ship fast, iterate faster. Feedback loops are compressed. Ideas become products in weeks, not months.
iii.
Systems thinking
Every product decision exists within a larger ecosystem. We design for scale from day one.

Honesty over enthusiasm
