Metro Cards

App-like landing grid

A startup homepage built like a product dashboard wall.

Instead of one hero and stacked sections, this design is a dense tile system. Visitors scan, compare, and discover the company through blocks of different weight.

Open system

Why it feels different

It borrows from operating systems, launchers, and media dashboards. The whole interface is the visual idea.

Strong fit

Analytics startups, AI workspaces, OS-like products, or any company that wants more density and less brochure behavior.

Scanable

Each tile can turn into a pricing box, KPI, founder note, or feature module.

UX shift

The user browses a surface, not a vertical story.

Production path

This pattern could grow into a real launchpad, docs home, or logged-in dashboard shell with very little structural change.
