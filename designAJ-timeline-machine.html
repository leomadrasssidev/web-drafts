Build the homepage as a time machine.

Instead of sections by feature, organize the startup by moments: why it started, what changed, what is happening now, and where it is going next. The whole UX is chronological.

Before

The world had a workflow problem, a bottleneck, or an old system worth replacing.

Now

The startup appears as the turning point, not just another product entry on a page.

Next

Strong for mission-driven brands, transformation stories, and founders with a thesis about where the market is headed.
